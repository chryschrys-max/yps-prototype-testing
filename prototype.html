How to Play

For the Teen

  1. Start by picking your wind-down magnets. What actually helps you feel relaxed before bed? Go with that.
  2. Same for the room setup. Think about what your ideal sleep environment looks like.
  3. Take a look at what your parent has written in their column. Does it feel fair? Have a chat if anything needs tweaking.
  4. Each night, just grab the feelings magnet and mark how your sleep went. It only takes a few seconds.
  5. After a week, sit down together and see what worked. Keep what helped, let go of what did not.

For the Parent

  1. Give your teen space to build their routine first. It works much better when it feels like their idea.
  2. Go through their picks together. The goal is something you both feel good about.
  3. Fill in your column too. What will you do to support them? A quick check-in, setting a reminder, anything that feels manageable.
  4. After a week, check in with curiosity rather than judgment. Ask how it felt, not just whether they stuck to it.

Hook U Up

Tap a deck to flip it over and see the goal

How to Play

For the teen:

  1. Have a browse through the 5 topics and see which one speaks to you right now.
  2. Find a friend who wants to take on the same topic. The more you are both into it, the better.
  3. Each day, flip the next card together and take on whatever challenge comes up.
  4. Upload your proof before midnight. A photo, a selfie, a quick note — whatever the card asks for.
  5. Get through all 14 days and you and your buddy could win an HPB prize.

For the parent:

  • Let them choose their own topic and buddy. The ownership is kind of the whole point.
  • Ask how their challenge went that day. Keep it light, like you are curious, not checking up.
  • When they hit a streak, celebrate it with them. That goes a long way.

How to Play

  1. Shuffle the category cards and put the Swap and Spark piles somewhere easy to reach.
  2. Take turns picking a card and reading it out loud. Both of you have a go at answering — parents included.
  3. Want to go deeper after a round? Throw down a Spark card. No pressure though.
  4. Feeling curious about how well you know each other? Play a Swap card and answer as the other person. Then compare.
  5. Go for as many rounds as feels right. If a question is not for you today, just pass and pick another one. No wrong answers.
