WOU (We Open Up)

Draw question cards and answer together. Build trust between parent and youth one card at a time.

How to Use

  1. Choose your wind-down routine — drag magnets from the tray onto the board. Agree on it together.
  2. Set up your room — pick room setup magnets. Your picks, both happy with it.
  3. Parent commits too — drag parent action magnets to the board. Youth reviews it.
  4. Track your sleep — every night, drag a feeling magnet to the day slot.
  5. Weekly check-in — after a week, sit down and talk about how it went. No pressure.
  6. Adjust — keep what works and drop what doesn't.

How to Use

  1. Pick a box — choose the topic that speaks to you both.
  2. Find your buddy — minimum 2 people, same box, same commitment.
  3. Daily challenge — each day, flip the next card in order and complete the challenge together.
  4. Scan & upload — scan the QR code and upload proof before midnight.
  5. Don't break the streak — miss a day and start over.
  6. Win! — complete all 14 days and enter the public gallery. HPB awards a prize to the most-voted pair.

How to Use

  1. Sit together — shuffle the category cards. Place Swap and Spark in separate piles.
  2. Take turns — draw a card and read it out loud. Both answer. Parent first or youth first — you decide.
  3. Spark cards — after any card, either person can draw a Spark card to keep going deeper.
  4. Swap cards — at any point, play a Swap card to answer as the other person. Find out how well you know each other.
  5. No pressure — play as many rounds as you like. If a question feels too personal, just say pass and draw another.
