How to Use

  1. Choose your wind-down routine — drag magnets from the tray onto the Wind-Down zone.
  2. Set up your room — drag room setup magnets to the Room Setup zone.
  3. Parent commits too — type what the parent will do in the Parent section.
  4. Track your sleep — drag a feeling face to each day slot.
  5. Weekly check-in — after a week, talk about how it went.
  6. Adjust — keep what works, drop what doesn't.

How to Use

  1. Pick a deck — choose the challenge topic that speaks to you both.
  2. Find your buddy — minimum 2 people, same deck, same commitment.
  3. Daily challenge — each day, flip the next card and complete the challenge together.
  4. Scan & upload — scan the QR code on the back and upload proof before midnight.
  5. Don't break the streak — miss a day and start over.
  6. Win! — complete all 14 days and enter the public gallery.

How to Use

  1. Shuffle the deck — hit shuffle to mix up all the cards.
  2. Pick a card — tap any face-down card to flip and reveal.
  3. Read & answer — both answer the question out loud. No judgement.
  4. Spark cards — go deeper on the last question. Keep the conversation flowing.
  5. Swap cards — answer as the other person. See how well you know each other.
  6. No pressure — play as many or as few rounds as you like.
