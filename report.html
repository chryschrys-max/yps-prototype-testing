Key Findings

1

High Engagement Across All Concepts

Participants consistently showed strong engagement with all three prototype concepts, averaging 87% active involvement throughout sessions.

2

Visual Design Resonates

The visual design language received positive feedback, with participants highlighting the clarity and appeal of the card-based layouts.

3

Interaction Patterns Clear

Users intuitively understood the interaction patterns, with minimal guidance needed for core navigation and card exploration flows.

4

Areas for Improvement

Some participants suggested clearer visual cues for interactive elements and improved mobile responsiveness for smaller screens.

♦

Summary & Next Steps

Overall Assessment

The prototype testing phase has yielded encouraging results across all three concepts. Participant feedback and engagement metrics indicate strong potential for further development. The next phase will focus on iterating based on specific feedback, conducting follow-up sessions with refined prototypes, and preparing final recommendations for stakeholder review.
